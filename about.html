About This Project

This website was created as part of my IT 3203 Web Development course at Kennesaw State University. The purpose of this project is to explore the evolution of artificial intelligence, from its early development to its modern applications. I chose this topic because artificial intelligence has become an increasingly more important part of technology and I wanted to better understand how it has developed and how it continues to influence different industries. Through this project, I am also applying the HTML and CSS skills I have learned in and outside of class to design and develop an informative and engaging website.
