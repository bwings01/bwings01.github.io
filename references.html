References and Resources

The following references and educational resources provide information about the history, development, concepts, and applications of artificial intelligence.

Research References

  1. Dartmouth College - Origins of Artificial Intelligence

    Explains the origins of AI and the 1956 Dartmouth Summer Research Project.

  2. IBM - Deep Blue

    Discusses IBM's Deep Blue computer and its historic chess match against Garry Kasparov.

  3. Encyclopaedia Britannica - Artificial Intelligence

    Provides an overview of artificial intelligence, including its history and development.

  4. FDA - AI-Enabled Medical Devices

    Provides information about artificial intelligence in medical devices and healthcare.

  5. UNESCO - Artificial Intelligence in Education

    Explores the opportunities and challenges associated with AI in education.

  6. NHTSA - Automated Vehicles for Safety

    Explains automated driving technologies, their potential benefits, and safety concerns.

Additional Learning Resources

  • IBM - What Is Machine Learning?

  • IBM - What Is Deep Learning?

    Describes deep learning, neural networks, and their role in modern AI.

  • Google - Machine Learning Crash Course

    Offers free lessons and interactive exercises covering machine learning concepts.

  • Computer History Museum

    Provides historical information about computing and technological developments.
